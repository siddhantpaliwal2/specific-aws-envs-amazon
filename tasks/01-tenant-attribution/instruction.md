Finance cannot reconcile instance uptime. A metered machine in an account dedicated to one customer belongs to that customer regardless of which customer its tags name, including when they name none. A shared-account machine may belong to several customers and its uptime must be divided evenly among tenants genuinely onboarded for that account; unknown or unplaceable usage stays off the bill. The onboarding records in the metering bucket beside the account list identify billable customers. Update the metering sweep so published uptime follows those rules. This task includes a local AWS-compatible emulator at the preconfigured AWS_ENDPOINT_URL. Its credentials and region are already in the shell, the repository's AWS SDK clients use it, and no real AWS account is involved. You may inspect its resources while developing and testing.
