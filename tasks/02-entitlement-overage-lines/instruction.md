Each customer's aggregated usage reaches the metric store by business, customer and dimension. Each business keeps a billing catalogue in the billing bucket with plans, dimensions, allowances, overage rules, invoice settings and customer assignments. The invoicing collector already reads both. Charge only permitted usage beyond a finite exhausted allowance; an unlimited or unexhausted allowance, or a plan that forbids overage, charges nothing. Give a dimension a line whenever the customer owes something on it, and give a dimension the plan prices at zero a line unless the invoice settings hide free dimensions. This task includes a local AWS-compatible emulator at the preconfigured AWS_ENDPOINT_URL. Its credentials and region are already in the shell, the repository's AWS SDK clients use it, and no real AWS account is involved. You may inspect its resources while developing and testing.
